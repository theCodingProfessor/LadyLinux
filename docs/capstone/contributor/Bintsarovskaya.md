### What did Anna contribute to the project?
Anna played a crucial role in designing the architecture of the LadyLinux system, particularly in integrating the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) framework with the LLM (Language Model) components. She worked on optimizing the data retrieval processes to ensure that the system could efficiently access and utilize external knowledge sources, which is essential for providing accurate and contextually relevant responses.
Anna also contributed to front-end intuitiveness by adding widget-based controls for starting and stopping the system, making it more user-friendly for non-technical users. Her work on the desktop widgets allowed users to easily manage the system without needing to interact with command-line interfaces, thus enhancing accessibility and usability.

### Where can I find Anna's contributions in the codebase?
Anna's contributions can be found in the following areas of the codebase:
- **RAG Integration**: Look for commits related to the implementation of the RAG framework, particularly in the `core/rag` responsible for data retrieval and LLM interaction.
- **Desktop Widgets**: Check the `scripts/` directory for the new `.desktop` files and the associated shell scripts that handle the start and stop functionalities of the system.
